📖 How to Use NexusPOS

  • Quick Sale (Always Visible): Use the right-hand panel to search for products. Click any product to add it to the cart. Click "Proceed to Checkout" to finalize the transaction and update database records.
  • Dashboard: View live revenue, transaction counts, and a 7-day sales trend graph. Critical low-stock warnings automatically appear here.
  • Sales: Review the complete ledger of past receipts. Click 'Print' on any row to generate a physical receipt copy.
  • Products: Monitor real-time inventory levels. Badges turn red when stock falls below healthy thresholds.
